What is in the basket

A family Saturday fire is more than meat — so from basket v2 the basket prices the whole thing: the meat, the fire, the table and the cooldrink. No ranking of shops, no “best price in SA” table. Citations exist so you can see where a figure came from. They are not a storefront. Planning 24 September, Heritage Day? The Braai Day page has the quantities, the wood and the timeline. This number is the published basket for the week written above it, not a till slip for the holiday.

    The method

    Each line is a single confirmed public guest price. We do not average two till slips and call it science. We do not fill a hole from last week. A gap stays a gap until someone sends a price we can publish.
