01 · The ignition

Every braai starts
with fire.

Kameeldoring from a permitted supplier, sekelbos, or a good bag of charcoal. Scroll to strike the match.

Wood & coals

02 · The perfect coal

Wait for the white.

Flames are for show. Coals are for cooking. When the ash coats them grey-white and the heat stings your palm at a hand’s height, the fire is ready.

Stand around the fire

03 · The sear

Then the meat meets
the heat.

A thick, well-marbled cut. Hot grid. Turn it once, maybe twice — and never, ever tell the braaier how to do it.
