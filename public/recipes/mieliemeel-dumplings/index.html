Mieliemeel braai dumplings

Maize-meal dumplings steamed on top of a simmering potjie — the old blog's cleverest trick, revived. They swell in the gravy's steam and arrive as free extra supper from ingredients you already had.

On the simmering potjie Makes ±12 35 minutes

Maize-meal dumplings steamed on top of a simmering potjie — the old blog's cleverest trick, revived. They swell in the gravy's steam and arrive as free extra supper from ingredients you already had.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup maize meal
  • 1 cup cake flour + 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp cold butter
  • ±200 ml milk

Method

  1. Whisk the maize meal, flour, baking powder and salt together; rub in the cold butter.
  2. Stir in just enough milk for a soft, sticky dough.
  3. Drop spoonfuls on top of a simmering potjie (or any stew at the fire), lid on.
  4. Steam 20–25 minutes. The lid stays ON — a peeked-at dumpling is a flat dumpling, and the potjie's no-peeking law applies in full.

Which fire, and which day? On a simmering potjie, lid closed. They are not a grid dish. Hold your palm at grid height and count: 4 seconds is hot, 6 is hot-medium, 8 is medium, 10 or more is gentle. Questions the fire actually gets

How do you cook mieliemeel dumplings?

Drop spoonfuls of the dough on a simmering potjie or any stew at the fire, lid on, for 20–25 minutes. They steam in the gravy. The lid stays on — a peeked-at dumpling is a flat one.

What is in the dough?

One cup maize meal, one cup cake flour, two teaspoons baking powder, one teaspoon salt, two tablespoons cold butter rubbed in, and about 200 ml milk — enough for a soft, sticky dough.

Do they go on the grid?

No. They need a covered pot with liquid underneath. The potjie guide has the no-peeking law these dumplings borrow.
