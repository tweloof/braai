Why Braai Day?

Heritage Day, 24 September, is the public holiday on which South Africans celebrate who we are — all of who we are. The National Braai Day idea grew from a simple observation: whatever our differences, the fire is where all of us already gather. It's the most South African form of unity there is — nobody has to agree on anything except that the coals are almost ready.

So the brief is simple: on 24 September, light a fire, gather your people, and feed them. This page is your planning kit.

The Braai Day timeline

TimeWhat's happening
09:00Meat collected. If you're only starting to look for wood now, may fortune favour you — everyone else bought theirs on Tuesday.
11:30Salads delegated, drinks on ice, dough for roosterkoek proving in the sun.
13:00Fire lit. The braai has now officially begun, whatever the guests think. Fire guide here.
13:45The pap goes on at the fire's edge. Smoor simmering. Hand test says "not yet".
14:00Hand test says go: sosaties and tjops on, then the wors.
14:45Meat rests under foil. Braaibroodjies take their rightful place over the dying coals.
15:00The nation eats. Sing the anthem if you know all the verses; hum with confidence if you don't.
SunsetThe second, purely ceremonial fire is lit. Stories exaggerate themselves. This is heritage too.

The menu, sorted

Feeding a crowd? Work on 400–500 g of meat per adult and let the classics carry you: boerewors rolls with tomato smoor as the anchor, chicken sosaties so nobody waits hungry, pap en sous to stretch everything, braaibroodjies because it isn't a braai without them, and roosterkoek if you're showing off. Dessert is a marshmallow on a stick or a peppermint tart from the fridge — this is not the day for plated desserts.

This year: the fire that must not go out

In the run-up to Braai Day 2026 there is one communal fire burning on this site — The Braai — fed by anyone with something worth putting on it. On 24 September we will also name the site's founding custodians right there: the people who will keep braai.co.za a community thing. Go stand around it.

The only to-do list that matters: wood (twice as much as you think), meat from a proper butcher, ice (three times as much as you think), matches, and people you like. Everything else is decoration.
