Ibhodwe okungafanele
lisukelwe neze.

I-potjiekos ingumzala obekezelayo we-braai: ibhodwe lensimbi elinezinyawo ezintathu, amalahle ambalwa, umthetho oqinile wezingqimba, namahora okwenza lutho ngobukhazikhazi. Amaresiphi amathathu akudala okwamanje asesiNgisi.

Ngokweqiniso: amaresiphi (inkukhu, ubhekeni namakhowe; ingulube ne-curry; inkomo newayini ebomvu) nomdwebo wezingqimba kukulona mhlahlandlela wesiNgisi wepotjie.

Ibhodwe

Amapotjie abalwa ngenombolo, hhayi ngokulinganisa. I-no. 2 yondla abantu abane ngokunethezeka, i-no. 3 (eyabantu) yondla abayisithupha kuya kwabayisishiyagalombili, nokukodwa okukhulu kungowemishado nezifinalis. Ibhodwe elisha liyanikwa amafutha: susa ukumbozwa kwasefekthri, ugcobe uwoyela, ulibhase libe mnyama kanye noma kabili. Ngemva kwalokho ulisule, uligcobe — insipho yamanye amabhodwe.

Umthetho wezingqimba

Okudinga isikhathi eside kuhlala phansi. Qala ngokuthosa inyama phansi ebhodweni, emafutha, ngamaqoqo. Thambisa u-anyanisi kulawo mafutha. Bese: imifino eqinile (ama-carrot, amazambane) ngaphambi eyethambile, namanzi aneleyo ukufutha umbhoshongo ngaphandle kokuwukhukhumeza. Ipotjie akusiyo isobho. Bese uvala isivalo.

Umthetho wokungasukeli

Awusukeli ipotjie. Hhayi ukuze ubheke, hhayi ukuze usize, hhayi ngoba unovalo. Ukusukela kuwisa izingqimba futhi kumemezela kubo bonke ukuthi wethukile. Okukodwa okuvunyelwe: ukugoqa kancane emizuzwini yokugcina uma iresiphi iqeda ngokhilimu noma ngonongo. Ngaphandle kwalokho isivalo sihlala sivaliwe.

Ipotjie ifuna amalahle ambalwa kakhulu kune-braai — umbhede omncane ngaphansi, njalo engxenye yehora amalahle amasha omlilweni oseceleni. Izinkuni nokuhlola kwesandla: Izinkuni Namalahle. Usuku Lwe-Braai ngoLwesine, 24 kuSepthemba 2026 — isethi yokuhlela.
