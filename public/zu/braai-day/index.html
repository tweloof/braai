Kungani Usuku Lwe-Braai?

Usuku Lwamagugu, u-24 kuSepthemba, yiholidi lomphakathi lapho abantu baseNingizimu Afrika begubha lokho esiyikho — konke esiyikho. Umqondo woSuku Lwe-Braai Lukazwelonke wakhula ekubukeni okulula: noma ngabe yini imehluko yethu, umlilo yilapho sonke sesivele sihlangana khona. Kuyindlela yobunye yaseNingizimu Afrika ngempela kunazo zonke — akekho okudingeka avumelane nalutho ngaphandle kokuthi amalahle asezolunga.

Ngakho umyalelo ulula: ngo-24 kuSepthemba, basa umlilo, qoqa abantu bakho, ubondle. Leli khasi liyisethi yakho yokuhlela.

Uhlelo lwesikhathi loSuku Lwe-Braai

IsikhathiOkwenzekayo
09:00Inyama ilandiwe. Uma usanda kuqala ukufuna izinkuni manje, sengathi inhlanhla ingaba nawe — bonke abanye bathenge ezabo ngoLwesibili.
11:30Amasaladi abelwe abanye, iziphuzo zisemakhazeni, inhlama ye-roosterkoek ikhukhumala elangeni.
13:00Umlilo ubasiwe. I-braai isiqalile ngokusemthethweni, noma ngabe izivakashi zicabangani. Umhlahlandlela womlilo ulapha.
13:45Uphuthu lufakwa onqenqemeni lomlilo. Isishebo siyabila kancane. Ukuhlola kwesandla kuthi "hhayi okwamanje".
14:00Ukuhlola kwesandla kuthi hamba: ama-sosatie nama-tjop ayafakwa, bese kuba yi-wors.
14:45Inyama iphumula ngaphansi kwefoyili. Ama-braaibroodjie athatha indawo yawo efanele phezu kwamalahle afayo.
15:00Isizwe siyadla. Cula iculo lesizwe uma uwazi wonke amavesi; huma ngokuzethemba uma ungawazi.
Ukushona kwelangaUmlilo wesibili, owomkhosi kuphela, uyabaswa. Izindaba ziyazikhulisa. Nalokhu kungamagugu.

Ukudla, kuhleliwe

Wondla isixuku? Sebenza ngo-400–500 g wenyama ngomuntu omdala futhi uvumele okwakudala kukuthwale: ama-boerewors roll nesishebo sikatamatisi njengesisekelo, ama-sosatie enkukhu ukuze kungabikho olinda elambile, u-phuthu nesishebo ukwelula konke, ama-braaibroodjie ngoba akusiyo i-braai ngaphandle kwawo, ne-roosterkoek uma uziqhayisa. Ukudla okumnandi kokugcina yi-marshmallow othini noma i-peppermint tart esiqandisini — akusilo usuku lwezidlo ezihlelwe ngobunono.

Kulo nyaka: umlilo okungafanele ucime

Endleleni eya oSukwini Lwe-Braai 2026 kukhona umlilo owodwa wesizwe ovutha kule webhusayithi — I-Braai — owondliwa yinoma ubani onokuthile okufanele kubekwe kuwo. Ngo-24 kuSepthemba sizokwazisa futhi abagcini abasunguli bale sayithi khona lapho: abantu abazogcina i-braai.co.za iyinto yomphakathi. Hamba uyowuzungeza.

Uhlu lokwenziwa okuwukuphela kwalo olubalulekile: izinkuni (okuphindwe kabili kunalokho okucabangayo), inyama evela kusilaha oqotho, iqhwa (okuphindwe kathathu kunalokho okucabangayo), omentshisi, nabantu obathandayo. Konke okunye kungumhlobiso.
