Umhlahlandlela wokuthenga · ngoLwesine, 24 kuSepthemba 2026

Yazi isilwane.
Bese ukhetha isitho.

Umhlahlandlela ophelele wokuthenga — inkomo, imvu, ingulube, inkukhu ne-boerewors, nezithombe — okwamanje usesiNgisi. Nakhu okufanele ukwazi ngaphambi kokufona usilaha.

Ngokweqiniso: leli khasi lichaza ingxenye. Imidwebo, izikhathi nethebula lesitho ngasinye kukulona mhlahlandlela wesiNgisi.

Yini ehamba kudosi

  • I-boerewors ethengiswa eNingizimu Afrika njenge-boerewors kumele ngokomthetho ibe yinyama okungenani engu-90%. Yosa yonke, uyiphendule kancane, ungayihlabi.
  • Thenga kugqinsiwe. I-steak engaphansi kuka-3 cm iba mpunga ngaphambi kokuba ivuthwe ngaphandle. I-rump, i-sirloin, i-ribeye ne-T-bone zihamba kudosi; i-brisket nentende zifuna amahora amanzi — leyo yipotjie.
  • Usuku Lwe-Braai, ngoLwesine 24 kuSepthemba 2026. Esixukwini: i-boerewors yangempela, amathanga enkukhu ama-sosatie, kanye nesitho esisodwa esiyisikhungo — umlenze wemvu ovuliwe noma intamo yengulube. Hlela u-400–500 g yenyama ngomuntu omdala. Ubuningi nohlelo lwesikhathi kuku-Suku Lwe-Braai.
  • Ukuhlola kwesandla, intende ebangeni likadosi: imizuzwana emi-4 kushisa, emi-6 kushisa maphakathi, emi-8 maphakathi, engu-10 noma ngaphezulu kancane. Ukufunda okuphelele kuku-Izinkuni Namalahle.

Umhlahlandlela ophelele ngesiNgisi

Ukulandelana koSuku Lwe-Braai — ama-sosatie ngosuku olwandulele, i-roosterkoek lapho ubasa, uphuthu ngenkathi amalangabi efa — kuse-khasini lamaresiphi.

Umhlahlandlela ophelele okwamanje usesiNgisi, ukuze amathebula nobuningi kungalahleki ekuhumusheni okusheshayo ngaphambi koSuku Lwe-Braai. Uma okuthile kungezwakali kahle, bhalela ku-lekker@braai.co.za.
