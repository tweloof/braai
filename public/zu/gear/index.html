Izinsiza

Amathuluzi amahle.
Amathuluzi ambalwa.

I-braai isinde isikhathi eside yamathuluzi ngokungawanaki. Umhlahlandlela ophelele — kuhlanganise nokuthi unikeza kanjani udosi owenziwe ngokulinganisa kwakho — okwamanje usesiNgisi. Nakhu udosi, ngoba uwuthinta ukudla.

Ngokweqiniso: umdwebo wezilinganiso nompikiswano omude kukulona mhlahlandlela wesiNgisi wezinsiza.

Udosi uqala

Konke emlilweni kuyaxoxiswana ngaphandle kukadosi. Yiyona nto eyodwa ethinta ukudla kwakho.

UdosiWenzelani
Udosi ovulekile oyisicabaYiwona osebenza kakhulu — i-steak, ama-tjop, i-wors ezintini uqobo.
I-toeklap-roosterAma-braaibroodjie, inhlanzi, noma yini ethambile. Phendula amabroodjie amaningi njengakunye.
Ukuphakama okushintshayoPhakamisa i-wors, yehlisa ukuze ivuthwe ngaphandle, ngaphandle kokususa amalahle.
Udosi wenhlanziI-snoek ephelele nezinye izinhlanzi ezivuliwe — seka umzimba wonke ukuze inyama ingaphuki. I-snoek ilele iyisicaba, isikhumba sibheke phansi.
Udosi owenziwe ngaweUma i-braai yakho ingusayizi ongajwayelekile. Insimbi engagqwali i-304, noma insimbi ethambile oyigcoba ngemuva komlilo ngamunye.

Uma uwenza

  • Izikhala zezinti: u-10–12 mm kudosi ojwayelekile — kuvale ngokwanele ukuze i-wors ingaweli, kuvuleke ngokwanele ukuhlanza.
  • Ubukhulu benthi: i-5–6 mm ibamba ukushisa futhi ayigobi ngaphansi komlenze wemvu.
  • Izibambo: zide kunalokho ocabangayo — okungenani u-300 mm, zibheke kancane phezulu, ukuze amakhwapha akho angabi sebangeni lamalahle.
