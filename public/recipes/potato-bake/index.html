Braai potato bake

The most argued-over side dish in the country, and one of the old braai.co.za's most-visited recipes. Creamy under a golden cheese lid, cooked at the fire while the meat gets the attention. The onion-soup sachet is optional but traditional — we don't make the rules.

Kettle braai or beside medium coals Serves 8 1 h 30

The most argued-over side dish in the country, and one of the old braai.co.za's most-visited recipes. Creamy under a golden cheese lid, cooked at the fire while the meat gets the attention. The onion-soup sachet is optional but traditional — we don't make the rules.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg potatoes, peeled and thinly sliced
  • 1 large onion, thinly sliced, and 2 cloves garlic
  • Salt, black pepper, a scrape of nutmeg
  • 250 ml cream + 250 ml milk
  • 1 sachet white onion soup (optional, classic)
  • 150 g cheddar, grated

Method

  1. Layer the potatoes, onion and garlic in a buttered foil dish or flat potjie, seasoning each layer.
  2. Whisk the cream, milk and soup sachet together and pour it over. Seal with foil.
  3. Bake 60–75 minutes in a kettle braai (or on a grid beside medium coals with coals on the lid) until a knife slides through without argument.
  4. Uncover, top with the cheddar, and give it 15 minutes more until golden and bubbling. Guard it.

Which fire, and which day? A kettle braai, or beside medium coals with a few coals on the lid. Not over open flame. Hold your palm at grid height and count: 4 seconds is hot, 6 is hot-medium, 8 is medium, 10 or more is gentle. The full reading is the hand test, and the wood that makes those coals is in the wood guide. Buying notes: the meat it shares the fire with. Wednesday 24 September is National Braai Day.

Questions the fire actually gets

Where do you cook a braai potato bake?

In a kettle braai, or on a grid beside medium coals with coals on the lid. Layer potatoes, onion and garlic in a buttered foil dish or flat potjie, seal with foil, and bake 60–75 minutes until a knife slides through.

Is the onion soup sachet required?

No. It is optional, and it is the classic version: whisked into 250 ml cream and 250 ml milk. Cheddar goes on for the last 15 minutes, uncovered, until golden.

How many people does it feed?

Eight, which is why it appears when the cousins do. Give it the side of the fire, and keep the direct coals for the meat.
