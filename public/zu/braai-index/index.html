Umlilo womndeni owashicilelwa kamuva ngoMgqibelo — inyama, umlilo, itafula nokuphuzwayo. Imithombo, hhayi isitolo.

Umgqomu v2 (kusukela ngomhla ka-31 August 2026): 1 kg ye-boerewors, 1 kg yama-chop entamo yengulube anethambo, 1 kg yepaki yenkukhu ye-braai; amalahle, izibasi zomlilo nama-mathisisi; amaroli, isaladi samazambane, ummbila ne-chakalaka; usawoti we-braai, isosi katamatisi nefoyili; kanye nokuphuzwayo elingu-2 litre. Inombolo eyodwa yomgqomu womndeni, eyesibili ne-6-pack ye-lager.
Ama-braai amathathu, itafula elilodwa: umlilo, okuhambisana, okungeziwe nokuphuzwayo kuhlala kufana — inyama kuphela eshintshayo. Ibhajethi: R644.87. Eyakudala: R690.87. Ephezulu: R955.87. I-6-pack ye-lager yengeza u-R99.99 kunoma iyiphi.

Yini esemgqomeni

Umlilo womndeni ngoMgqibelo ungaphezu kwenyama — ngakho kusukela kumgqomu v2 umgqomu ubiza konke: inyama, umlilo, itafula nokuphuzwayo. Ayikho inhlwankomo yezitolo. Imithombo ikhona ukuze ubone ukuthi inombolo ivela kuphi. Akusona isitolo. Uhlela uLwesine, 24 kuSepthemba 2026? Ikhasi lo-Suku Lwe-Braai linobuningi, izinkuni nohlelo lwesikhathi. Le nombolo ngumgqomu oshicilelwe weviki elibhalwe ngaphezulu, hhayi irisidi yeholide.

Amagama emigqa, amagama ezitolo kanye nelebula leviki avela efayeleni lesiNgisi. Asihumushi irandi.

    Indlela

    Umugqa ngamunye uyintengo eyodwa yomphakathi eqinisekisiwe. Asibali isilinganiso sezinkontileko ezimbili bese sikubiza ukuthi isayensi. Asigcwalisi isikhala ngeviki eledlule. Isikhala sihlala isikhala kuze kuthunyelwe intengo esingayishicilela. Umgqomu v2 ubiza umlilo wonke, hhayi inyama kuphela: amakhilogremu amathathu enyama (1 kg ye-boerewors, 1 kg yama-chop entamo yengulube anethambo, 1 kg yepaki yenkukhu), umlilo uqobo (ama-briquette amalahle angu-4 kg, izibasi zomlilo, ama-mathisisi), itafula (amaroli ayi-6, isaladi samazambane, ummbila, i-chakalaka), okungeziwe (usawoti we-braai, isosi katamatisi, ifoyili) nokuphuzwayo elingu-2 litre. Kukhona izinombolo ezimbili: i-braai yomndeni, nomgqomu ofanayo ne-6-pack ye-lager. Izintengo zokuthengisa ziyabala. Eyebhajethi neyephezulu zishintsha inyama kuphela. Indlela yesiNgisi efayeleni lomthombo yiyona eqinisekile; le ndima yimithetho efanayo.
