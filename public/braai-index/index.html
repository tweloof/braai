What is in the basket

A family Saturday fire is more than meat — so from this week the basket prices the whole thing: the meat, the fire, the table and the cooldrink. No ranking of shops, no “best price in SA” table. Citations exist so you can see where a figure came from. They are not a storefront.

    The method

    Each line is a single confirmed public guest price. We do not average two till slips and call it science. We do not fill a hole from last week. A gap stays a gap until someone sends a price we can publish.

    Gaps this week

      Got a public guest price for a missing line — a shelf ticket, a weekly specials page anyone can open without an account? Send it to lekker@braai.co.za with the shop, the town, the date and a photo of the ticket. An agent or a person will check it.
