The Braaictionary.

Speak braai fluently. The essential vocabulary first, then the slang — because half of braai culture lives in the language.

The Braaictionary began life on this site in June 2009 as a dictionary built by its readers, one submitted word at a time. This is its modern edition: the vocabulary you actually need at a South African fire, followed by a selection of the wordplay tradition the original was loved for — rewritten, curated, and offered in the same spirit.

The essential vocabulary

TermSay itMeaning
braaibryThe fire, the event, the equipment and the act of cooking on it. Noun and verb. The reason you're here.
braaivleisbry-flayss"Grilled meat" — the older, fuller form of the word, and the meat itself.
braaier / braaimeesterbry-erThe one holding the tongs; the meester is the one whose fire you don't touch without permission.
boerewors / wors / boerieboo-ruh-vorssThe coriander-spiced farmer's sausage, at least 90% meat by law. Know the real thing.
tjopchopA chop, usually lamb. The plural is "not enough".
sosatiesoh-sah-teeA skewer of marinated meat — Cape Malay heritage. Recipe here.
braaibroodjiebry-brue-keyCheese, tomato, onion and chutney, toasted slowly in a hinged grid. The braaier's true exam. Recipe here.
roosterkoekroo-ster-cookBread rolls baked on the grid. Recipe here.
skilpadjieskil-pie-keyLamb's liver wrapped in caul fat, braaied over coals. Not the tortoise from the garden.
pofadderpawf-udderThe skilpadjie's big cousin — a coil of seasoned liver braaied like wors. Traditional, glorious, unapologetic.
stokbroodstock-broo-ertDough twisted around a stick and baked over coals — the children's department of the braai.
pap / stywe pap / slap pap / krummelpappupMaize porridge: firm, soft, or crumbly. The braai's great equaliser. Recipe here.
soussoceThe tomato-and-onion relish that turns pap into pap en sous. Also, regrettably, the weather that interferes with a braai.
chakalakachuck-a-luckaThe spicy vegetable relish — onion, pepper, carrot, beans, curry spice — that wakes up any plate.
monkey gland sauce—Sweet-tangy steakhouse sauce of onion, tomato, chutney and Worcestershire. Contains neither monkeys nor glands. Recipe here.
potjie / potjiekospoy-keyThe three-legged pot and the layered slow food made in it. Never stirred. Full guide here.
shisa nyamashee-sa nyah-maFrom Zulu, "burn the meat" — the township braai tradition: buy your meat at the butchery, braai it right there, music included. Braai culture at full volume.
flattieflat-eeA spatchcocked chicken, flattened for the grid.
varknekfark-neckPork neck — the most underrated cut in the country. See The Cuts.
toeklap-roostertoo-klup roo-sterThe hinged grid that clamps shut, for broodjies, fish and wors.
blitzblitsFirelighters, whatever the brand — and the eternal question: "did anyone bring blitz?"
kameeldoring / sekelbos / rooikrans—The holy trinity of braai wood. The full wood guide.
bring en braai—Bring your own meat and a side; the fire is provided. Note: "bring" refers to the meat, not merely yourself.
chop en dop—The minimalist format: one chop, one drink, zero admin.
dopdawpA drink. Keeps the braaier hydrated and the stories improving.
kuierkay-erTo visit, linger and talk for hours. The braai's actual purpose; the meat is the excuse.
spitbraaispit-bryA whole lamb or large cut on a rotating spit — the crowd-feeding format of weddings and matric farewells.
skottelskaw-tulThe gas disc pan of campsite breakfasts. Useful, beloved, and not a braai — we've checked.
Vrystaathamburger—Two slabs of stywe pap with the filling in between. The Free State does not explain itself.
lekkerlack-erGood, delicious, excellent, correct. The one-word review every braaier is working toward.

The braaictionary proper — slang & wordplay

The original 2009 braaictionary's real genius was its wordplay, submitted by readers over the years. A curated revival of the tradition:

EntryDefinition
abbraaiviationA very short braai.
braaiathonA very long one. Often starts as an abbraaiviation.
log onTo add wood and make the fire hotter.
log offTo remove wood and calm it down. IT support for fires.
braai passengerThe guest who arrives at a bring en braai having interpreted "bring" as referring only to themselves.
roofbraaierThe one who arrives with budget meat, braais it next to your dry-aged rump, and confidently plates your rump.
braaistardThe one who drinks your drink while you're minding the fire.
skilpadbraaiA braai with much dop and very little vleis — more drinking than braaiing gets done.
tan 'n tjopA deliberately slow braai, typically scheduled around an overseas rugby kick-off.
tjopswaaiTo braai; literally to swing chops about, preferably with a dop in the other hand.
kameeldrolleAffectionate slang for charcoal briquettes. Look at one and you'll see it.
clutchplaatMeat that stayed on the grid until it qualified as an automotive part.
doek sagMeat so tender it barely needed the fire's permission.
jammerlappieThe little cloth you apologise to after wiping your hands on it, having eaten wors like a civilised person.
Smokie RobertsonAn amateur fire-starter producing far more smoke than flame. Every braai has one; make sure it isn't you — the fire guide helps.
krismisbraaiThe braai most of South Africa has on the 25th of December, weather forecast: guaranteed.
referendum braaiA braai at which everyone present holds a different, strongly expressed view on how to braai. Resolved by consensus, eventually, over dops.
wasistmitbier?Frequently asked question among the braai team, ensuring adequate hydration of all personnel. (This one survives from the original unimproved, because it cannot be improved.)
