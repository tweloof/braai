Le webhusayithi yakhelwe
eduze komlilo.

Ngo-2009 i-Braaictionary yabhalwa abafundi, igama ngegama. Sikubuyisa lokho: sithumele iresiphi yomndeni wakho, igama esilishodayo, indaba ye-braai noma ithiphu ezuzwe kanzima. Konke okushicilelwayo kunakekelwa kufike ezingeni lale sayithi — futhi kubongwa wena ngegama lakho.

Kusebenza kanjani: uyathumela, umhleli afunde ngayinye, futhi okuhle kakhulu kubhalwa kabusha ngesitayela sasekhaya kushicilelwe negama lakho kukho. Izithombe? Thumela indaba kuqala — sizophendula nge-imeyili ukuze ukwazi ukunamathisela izithombe.

Ngokuthumela unikeza i-braai.co.za imvume yokushicilela inguqulo ehleliwe, kubongwa wena.
