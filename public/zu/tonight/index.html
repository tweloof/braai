Namuhla ebusuku · i-SAST

Ubani owasayo namuhla ebusuku?

Ukuthinta okukodwa. Idolobha lakho. Awukho i-akhawunti, awukho umakhelwane owenzelwe eSandton. Uma ubasa umlilo, sho. Phakathi kwamabili nge-SAST kusulwa. Ibalazwe eliphilayo uqobo liseSingisini okwamanje. Usuku Lwe-Braai ngoLwesine, 24 kuSepthemba 2026.

Ngokweqiniso: leli khasi lichaza umdlalo. Ukuthinta, uhlu lwamadolobha nokubala okuphilayo kukulona Tonight ngesiNgisi. Ukubala okufanayo, phakathi kwamabili okufanayo.

Imithetho

  • Awekho ama-akhawunti. Asigcini igama lakho. Sibala amadolobha, hhayi abantu.
  • Abantu abenzelwe abakho, omakhelwane be-AI abakho. Ukuthinta ngumuntu kusiphequluli.
  • Ukuthinta okukodwa okweqiniso. Umsebenzi ukhawulela uxhumano; ifoni yakho nayo ikhumbula usuku.
  • Phakathi kwamabili nge-SAST — 00:00 eGoli, eKapa naseThekwini — kusulwa usuku. Kusasa ngumlilo omusha.

Sho kulebalazwe lesiNgisi

Uhlela uLwesine, 24 kuSepthemba? Isethi yoSuku Lwe-Braai inobuningi, izinkuni nohlelo lwesikhathi. Okubiza umgqomu womndeni oshicilelwe yi-Braai Index — akuyona intengo yoholide, kuze kube khona iviki elisha efayeleni.

Umhlahlandlela ophelele okwamanje usesiNgisi, ukuze amathebula nobuningi kungalahleki ekuhumusheni okusheshayo ngaphambi koSuku Lwe-Braai. Uma okuthile kungezwakali kahle, bhalela ku-lekker@braai.co.za.
