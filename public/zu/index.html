I-Braai iyavutha

Umlilo owodwa wesizwe sonke, ophilayo kule webhusayithi. Wehla njalo ngehora futhi uhlala uvutha kuphela uma othile ebeka okuthile kuwo — igama, indawo ye-braai, ulwazi lwezinkuni, iresiphi yomndeni. Uma ucima, ikhasi lihlala unomphela.

Imithetho Yomlilo

Izizukulwane ingabhaliwe. Ekugcineni ibhaliwe phansi.

  1. Owosayo uyena owosayo. Ophethe udosi nguye onezwi. Iseluleko singanikezwa kanye, ubukude obuhloniphekile, hhayi kabili.
  2. I-braai iqala lapho umlilo ubaswa, hhayi lapho inyama ifakwa. Amahora aphakathi akusikho ukulinda — ayiyona injongo uqobo.
  3. Izinkuni zenza amalahle; amalahle enza i-braai. Amalangabi angawombukiso. Inyama ihlangana nodosi kuphela lapho umlilo usuthulile futhi usukhulile.
  4. Themba ukuhlola kwesandla. Bamba intende yesandla ngaphezu kwamalahle ebangeni likadosi: imizuzwana eyisishiyagalombili ilungele i-wors, eyisithupha ama-tjop, emine kusho ukuthi linda.
  5. I-boerewors iphendulwa kancane futhi ayihlatshwa neze. Uma uhlaba isikhumba, amanzi enyama owalahlekelwayo ayikunambitheka obuthembise wonke umuntu ngakho.
  6. I-braaibroodjie akusiyo into eseceleni. Iyisilinganiso sowosayo. Amalahle aphansi, ukubekezela, nebhotela ngaphandle — njalo ngaphandle.
  7. Uphuthu nesishebo kondla isizwe. Ibhodwe lophuthu nesishebo sikatamatisi no-anyanisi kwenza ipakethe le-wors libe yidili labantu abayishumi nambili.
  8. Isimo sezulu silihemuhemu. Imvula isusa izivakashi zingene endlini; ayiwususi umlilo. Yingakho kukhona igaraji, uvulande nesambulela.
  9. Ungalokothi ubale ama-tjop. Kuhlala kukhona indawo yomunye umuntu e-braai, futhi ngandlela thile kuhlala kukhona elinye i-tjop kudosi.
  10. Umlilo uyehla; izindaba azehli. Ihora lokugcina eduze kwamalahle, emva kokudla, yilapho i-braai yenzeka khona ngempela.
