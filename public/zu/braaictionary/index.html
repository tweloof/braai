I-Braaictionary.

Khuluma i-braai kahle. Amagama abalulekile alaph. Ulwimi lwezindlela kanye nohlu oluphelele — olwavuswa kwisichazamazwi esakhiwa ngabafundi ngoJuni 2009 — okwamanje lusesiNgisi.

Ngokweqiniso: lolu uhlu olufushane. I-Braaictionary ephelele inakho konke, kuhlanganise namagama angamahlaya.

Amagama owadinga emlilweni

IgamaLisho ukuthini
braaiLiphinyiswa “bry”. Umlilo, umcimbi, izinsiza, nesenzo. Igama elilodwa lezinto ezintathu.
braaivleisIndlela endala, ephelele: inyama eyosiwe, nenyama uqobo.
boereworsIsoseji enesinongo se-coriander. Ngokomthetho iyinyama okungenani engu-90%. Yonke, iphendulwa kancane, ayihlatshwa.
tjopI-chop, ngokuvamile eyemvu. Iningi lithi “akwanele”.
rooster / toeklap-roosterUdosi we-braai. I-toeklap ngudosi onamahinge wama-braaibroodjie nenhlanzi.
potjie / potjiekosIbhodwe lezinyawo ezintathu, nokudla okukulo. Akusukelwa.
shisa nyamaIsiZulu, “shisa inyama”: uthenga kusilaha bese uyosa khona.
ukuhlola kwesandlaIntende ebangeni likadosi. Imizuzwana emi-4 kushisa, emi-6 kushisa maphakathi, emi-8 maphakathi, engu-10 noma ngaphezulu kancane.
