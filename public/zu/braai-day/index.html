Kungani Usuku Lwe-Braai?

Usuku Lwamagugu, u-24 kuSepthemba, yiholidi lomphakathi lapho abantu baseNingizimu Afrika begubha lokho esiyikho — konke esiyikho. Ngo-2026 luwela ngoLwesine. Umqondo woSuku Lwe-Braai Lukazwelonke wakhula ekubukeni okulula: noma ngabe yini imehluko yethu, umlilo yilapho sonke sesivele sihlangana khona. Kuyindlela yobunye yaseNingizimu Afrika ngempela kunazo zonke — akekho okudingeka avumelane nalutho ngaphandle kokuthi amalahle asezolunga.

Ngakho umyalelo ulula: ngo-24 kuSepthemba, basa umlilo, qoqa abantu bakho, ubondle. Leli khasi liyisethi yakho yokuhlela. Akukho lutho lapha oluthengisayo.

Kuleli khasi: okufanele ukuthenge, izinkuni, uhlelo lwesikhathi, ukudla, amaphutha, uma lina, kanye nemibuzo.

Okufanele ukuthenge, nokuthi kungakanani

Sebenza kusukela kubantu, hhayi kumenyu eyiphupho. Inani elilodwa leli khasi elilibeka yinyama: 400–500 g ngomuntu omdala. Abantu abadala abayisishiyagalombili cishe u-3.2–4 kg. Yixube ukuze abalambile babe nokuthile kusenesikhathi, nabaziqhayisayo babe nama-tjop. I-bring-and-braai isho ukuthi izivakashi ziziletha ingxenye yaleyo nyama — umsingathi usaziletha umlilo.

Okubize ngempela umgqomu womndeni oshicilelwe — inyama, umlilo, okuhambisana nokuphuzwayo, hhayi ukuqagela — kuhlala ku-National Braai Index (okwamanje ngesiNgisi kuphela). Iviki lakamuva efayeleni ngu-31 August – 6 September 2026, umgqomu v2, ophelele: R690.87 komlilo womndeni, R790.86 nepaki yezinhlanu zokuphuza. Lelo viki yilona izintengo ezaqinisekiswa ngalo. Akusona isitolo, futhi akuyona intengo ka-24 kuSepthemba kuze kushicilelwe iviki elisha lapho.

Izinkuni, ngaphambi kokuba uzidinge

Izinkuni eziqinile zivutha zibe amalahle ahlala isikhathi eside. I-kameeldoring (camel thorn) iyigolide futhi isihlahla esivikelwe — yithenge kumthengisi ovunyelwe. I-sekelbos yintandokazi yasemaphandleni. I-rooikrans iyisebenzi yaseKapa, futhi isitshalo esingafuneki, ngakho ukusishisa kuyisinyathelo esincane sokulondoloza. Izinkuni eziqinile ezinhle zidinga imizuzu engu-45–60 ukuba zibe amalahle. Uma amalangabi esaluma udosi, unomlilo, awukabi ne-braai. Ukuhlola kwesandla, intende ebangeni likadosi: imizuzwana emi-4 kushisa, emi-6 kushisa maphakathi, emi-8 maphakathi, engu-10 kancane. Ama-briquette ayisisekelo esifanele uma izikhwama seziphelile; igesi ilula lwangoLwesibili, hhayi umlilo woSuku Lwamagugu. Ukuqhathanisa okuphelele ku-mhlahlandlela wezinkuni namalahle.

Uhlelo lwesikhathi loSuku Lwe-Braai

Lezi zikhathi zithatha ukuthi ufuna ukudla cishe ngo-15:00. Basa izinkuni eziqinile ngo-13:00 ukuze zibe naleyo mizuzu engu-45–60. I-braai iqala lapho umlilo ubaswa, hhayi lapho inyama ifakwa.

Ukudla, kuhleliwe

Isabelomali senyama esifanayo esingenhla — 400–500 g ngomuntu omdala — bese uvumela okwakudala kukuthwale. Lawa amakhasi asekhona. Akukho okusha okusungelwe lo mcimbi.

  • Ama-boerewors roll nesishebo sikatamatisi — isisekelo.
  • Ama-sosatie enkukhu — afakwe kusenesikhathi, ukuze kungabikho olinda elambile.
  • Ama-tjop emvu, noma umlenze ovuliwe uma wondla isixuku.
  • U-phuthu nesishebo — yile ndlela inyama elula ngayo.
  • Ama-braaibroodjie phezu kwamalahle afayo, ibhotela ngaphandle. Akusiso isidlo esiseceleni. Isivivinyo.
  • I-roosterkoek, uma inhlama yaphuma ngo-11:30.
  • Ama-ribbetjie emvu noma izimbambo zengulube uma umuntu enokubekezela.
  • Ogwini: i-snoek, ivuliwe, iyisicaba, isikhumba sibheke phansi.
  • I-potato bake etafuleni, nama-bhanana kashokoledi lapho amalahle efa. Noma i-peppermint tart esiqandisini. Akusilo usuku lwezidlo ezihlelwe ngobunono.

Ishala lonke lama-resiphi ayishumi nanhlanu. I-potjie uma umuntu ehlala ntambama futhi ethembekile ukuthi akazukusukela.

Amaphutha asalela udosi uluze

  1. Inyama emalangabini. Amalangabi angowombukiso. Amalahle enza i-braai. Uma umlilo usaluma udosi, usheshe kakhulu.
  2. Ukuhlaba i-wors. Amanzi owalahlekelayo ayinambithela obayithembisile wonke umuntu. Udosi, hhayi imfoloko.
  3. Ama-braaibroodjie emlilweni oshisayo. Afuna amalahle aphansi, nebhotela ngaphandle — njalo ngaphandle.
  4. Izinkuni ezithengwe ekuseni ngoLwesine. Uma Usuku Lwe-Braai kunguLwesine, uLwesithathu wusuku lwezinkuni.
  5. Ukubala ama-tjop. Thenga onqenqemeni oluphezulu luka-400–500 g ngomuntu omdala, bese uyeka izibalo kudosi.
  6. Ukucindezela i-steak. Lokho kukhipha ingxenye oyikhokhelile.
  7. Ukukhansela ngenxa yesimo sezulu. Umlilo awungeni endlini. Izivakashi ziyangena. Bheka ngezansi.

Uma isimo sezulu singahloniphi

Isimo sezulu yindaba, hhayi umthetho. Imvula ihambisa izivakashi, hhayi umlilo. Umnyango wegaraji uvuliwe, isitezi, noma isambulela esikhulu. Gcina udosi ungaphandle kokumanzi uma ungakwazi. Qaphela umoya ngaphambi kokubasa, futhi ugcine umlilo ukude nezindlu zotshani namagatsha alengayo. Umlotha uhlala e-braai uze ubande njengetshe. Umhlahlandlela womlilo unamanothi okuphepha aphelele.

Izindawo ezintathu zokuma ngenkathi ulinda

  • Tonight — uma ubasa umlilo, sho ukuthi yidolobha liphi. Ukuthinta okukodwa, awukho i-akhawunti. Phakathi kwamabili nge-SAST kusulwa. Ikhasi okwamanje lisesiNgisi kuphela.
  • National Braai Index — umgqomu womndeni wakamuva oshicilelwe, neviki libhalwe kuwo. Akusona isitolo. Okwamanje ngesiNgisi kuphela.
  • I-Braai — umlilo womphakathi kule sayithi. Abagcini abasunguli bazokwaziswa lapho ngosuku, hhayi lapha.

Kulo nyaka: umlilo okungafanele ucime

Endleleni eya oSukwini Lwe-Braai 2026 kukhona umlilo owodwa wesizwe ovutha kule webhusayithi — I-Braai — owondliwa yinoma ubani onokuthile okufanele kubekwe kuwo. Abagcini abasunguli bazokwaziswa kulelo khasi ngo-24 kuSepthemba 2026. Ababhalwa kuleli khasi, futhi ababhalwa ngaphambi kosuku. Uma ufuna ukuphakamisa isandla ngaphambi kwalokho, ikhasi lomlilo lisho ukuthi kanjani. Hamba uyowuzungeza noma kunjalo.

Yini Usuku Lwe-Braai Lukazwelonke?

Usuku Lwe-Braai Lukazwelonke wusuku lapho abantu baseNingizimu Afrika, ngamabomu, njengezwe, bebasa umlilo bahlangane eduze kwawo. Luwela ngoSuku Lwamagugu. Umqondo ulula: noma ngabe yini esiphikisana ngayo, umlilo yilapho sesivele sihlangana khona. Leli khasi liyisethi yokuhlela — ubuningi, izinkuni, uhlelo lwesikhathi nokudla.

Ingabe Usuku Lwe-Braai Lukazwelonke lufana noSuku Lwamagugu?

Usuku olulodwa, amagama amabili. Usuku Lwamagugu yiholidi laseNingizimu Afrika ngo-24 kuSepthemba. Usuku Lwe-Braai Lukazwelonke yindlela abantu abaningi baseNingizimu Afrika abakhetha ukuluchitha ngayo: umlilo owodwa, abantu obathandayo, nokudla okwanele. Iholidi yiSuku Lwamagugu. I-braai ngumgubho.

Unini Usuku Lwe-Braai Lukazwelonke 2026?

NgoLwesine, 24 kuSepthemba 2026. Ukubala kuleli khasi kuya kuze kube sekuqaleni kwalolo suku, naphakathi nosuku uqobo. Ngonyaka olandelayo luphinde luwele ngo-24 kuSepthemba — usuku aluhambi.

Kudingeka inyama engakanani ngomuntu e-braai?

Hlela u-400–500 g wenyama ngomuntu omdala. Abantu abadala abayisishiyagalombili cishe baba u-3.2–4 kg, kuxutshwe ukuze kungabikho omile elambile: i-wors, okuthile okusheshayo njengama-sosatie, nama-tjop. Uphuthu, amaroli nama-braaibroodjie kuyelula. I-bring-and-braai isho ukuthi izivakashi ziziletha ingxenye yenyama.

Yiziphi izinkuni okufanele ngizisebenzise ngoSuku Lwe-Braai?

Izinkuni eziqinile ezivutha zibe amalahle ahlala isikhathi eside: i-kameeldoring evela kumthengisi ovunyelwe — isihlahla esivikelwe — i-sekelbos emaphandleni, ne-rooikrans eKapa, lapho ukushisa leso sitshalo esingafuneki kuyisinyathelo esincane sokulondoloza. Izinkuni eziqinile ezinhle zidinga imizuzu engu-45–60 ngaphambi kokuba zibe amalahle. Ama-briquette ayisisekelo esifanele uma izinkuni seziphelile. Zithenge ngosuku olwandulela. Ekuseni komhla ka-24, bonke abanye sebenazo.

Ngihlela kanjani uhlelo lwesikhathi loSuku Lwe-Braai?

Sebenza ubuyele emuva ekudleni cishe ngo-15:00. Landa inyama ekuseni. Basa umlilo ngo-13:00 ukuze izinkuni eziqinile zibe amalahle. Uphuthu lufakwa onqenqemeni cishe ngo-13:45. Lapho ukuhlola kwesandla kuthi hamba — cishe ngo-14:00 — ama-sosatie nama-tjop, bese kuba yi-wors. Phumuza inyama ngaphansi kwefoyili ngenkathi ama-braaibroodjie ethatha amalahle afayo. I-braai iqala lapho umlilo ubaswa, hhayi lapho inyama ifakwa.

Kwenziwa njani uma lina ngoSuku Lwe-Braai?

Isimo sezulu yindaba, hhayi umthetho. Imvula ihambisa izivakashi, hhayi umlilo. Umnyango wegaraji uvuliwe, isitezi, noma isambulela esikhulu. Gcina udosi ungaphandle kokumanzi uma ungakwazi. Amalahle awakavoti.
