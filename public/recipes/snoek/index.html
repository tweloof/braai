Snoek with apricot glaze

The West Coast's gift to the braai. A whole butterflied snoek, apricot-butter basting, and the smell that has sold a million weekend trips to Langebaan.

Medium coals · 8-second hand test Serves 6 35 minutes

The West Coast's gift to the braai. A whole butterflied snoek, apricot-butter basting, and the smell that has sold a million weekend trips to Langebaan.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole butterflied snoek (1.5–2 kg), fresh or properly thawed
  • 125 ml smooth apricot jam
  • 60 g butter
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Melt the jam, butter, lemon juice and garlic together at the side of the fire. Season the fish.
  2. Lay the snoek skin-side down in a well-oiled fish grid. It braais skin-side down for nearly the whole time — the skin protects the delicate flesh.
  3. Braai over medium coals for 20–25 minutes, basting generously every few minutes. Flip flesh-side down only for the last minute or two, for colour.
  4. It's done when the thickest part flakes with a fork. Serve with roosterkoek and soetpatats.

Which fire, and which day? Medium coals, the 8-second hand test. Hold your palm at grid height and count: 4 seconds is hot, 6 is hot-medium, 8 is medium, 10 or more is gentle. The full reading is the hand test, and the wood that makes those coals is in the wood guide. Buying notes: the snoek row in the time table. Thursday 24 September is National Braai Day.

Questions the fire actually gets

Which way up do you braai snoek?

Skin-side down for nearly the whole time, in a well-oiled fish grid. The skin protects the flesh. Flip flesh-side down only for the last minute or two, for colour.

What heat and how long for snoek?

Medium coals, the 8-second hand test, 20–25 minutes, basting often with apricot jam, butter, lemon and garlic melted together. It is done when the thickest part flakes with a fork.

Fresh or frozen snoek?

A whole butterflied snoek of 1.5–2 kg, fresh or properly thawed. Season it, then baste. This page serves it with roosterkoek.
