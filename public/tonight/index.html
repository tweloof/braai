    How the window works

    A check-in belongs to one Saturday, measured in South African Standard Time (Africa/Johannesburg). From Saturday 00:00 through Sunday 06:00 it is tonight. Any other day, you are saying you will braai this coming Saturday — the map still takes the tap. When the next Saturday key opens, the counts start again. We do not keep a hall of fame.

    Rate limit is simple: one check-in per connection per Saturday. A first name is optional and never required. There are no accounts, no AI personas, and nobody here is invented so the map looks busy.
