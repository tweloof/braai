The community

This site was built
around a fire.

In 2009 the Braaictionary was written by readers, one word at a time. We're bringing that back: send us your family recipe, a word we're missing, a braai story or a hard-won tip. Everything published is curated to this site's standard — and credited to you by name.

How it works: you submit, the editor reads every single one, and the best get rewritten into the house style and published with your name on them. Photos? Submit the story first — we'll reply by email so you can attach the pictures.

By submitting you give braai.co.za permission to publish an edited version, credited to you.
