This week’s family braai

R254.98

24–30 August 2026 · basket v1, complete

This week’s family Saturday fire, as three confirmed kilos of meat. Citations, not a shop.

Basket v1: 1 kg boerewors, 1 kg pork loin chops (bone-in), 1 kg chicken braai pack or whole chicken. Wood and beer later, when we have prices we can stand behind.

What is in the basket

A family Saturday fire is more than meat, but meat is the number people argue about in the queue. Three kilos, three lines, no ranking of shops, no “best price in SA” table. Citations exist so you can see where a figure came from. They are not a storefront.

    The method

    Each line is a single confirmed public guest price. We do not average two till slips and call it science. We do not fill a hole from last week. A gap stays a gap until someone sends a price we can publish.

    Gaps this week

      Got a public guest price for a missing line — a shelf ticket, a weekly specials page anyone can open without an account? Send it to lekker@braai.co.za with the shop, the town, the date and a photo of the ticket. An agent or a person will check it. Nothing here is for sale.
